Welcome!

Hello! I am Nishant Agarwal, a PhD Scholar in the Department of Materials Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jodhpur.

My research focuses on the computational design and optimization of next-generation perovskite solar cells, with particular emphasis on lead-free perovskite materials, tandem solar cells, and photovoltaic device simulations.

Research Interests

  • Perovskite Solar Cells
  • Lead-Free Double Perovskites
  • Tandem Solar Cells
  • Computational Materials Science
  • Renewable Energy

This website serves as a platform to share my research, publications, academic activities, and professional achievements.

About Me

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Materials Engineering at IIT Jodhpur.

My research focuses on computational modeling and simulation of next-generation perovskite solar cells using SCAPS-1D.

My research interests include:

  • Perovskite Solar Cells
  • Tandem Solar Cells
  • Computational Materials Science
  • Renewable Energy
